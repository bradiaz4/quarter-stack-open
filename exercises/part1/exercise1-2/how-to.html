How to Register a Child in a School

Registering a child in school is a short process with a few clear steps. This guide explains what to do before you go, what to bring, what happens at the school office, and what to do afterwards. Rules differ between schools and regions, so confirm the details with your school.

Before you go

A little preparation saves you from making a second trip. Take a day or two to find the right school and confirm how it accepts new students.

Find the right school

Choose a school close to your home, since many public schools give priority to children who live nearby. Visit the school or call the school administrative office to learn which schools serve your area and whether they have space in the class your child needs.

Check the registration dates

Most schools register new students during a short period before the school year begins. Ask for the opening and closing dates, the office hours, and whether you need to book an appointment.

What to bring

Schools need proof of who your child is, where you live, and what your child has already learned. Bring the original and one photocopy of each: your child's birth certificate, your own ID as parent or guardian, proof of address such as passport-size photos of your child. If your child is changing schools, also bring the last report card or a transfer letter, and any vaccination record.

At the office

Go to the school's administration office, usually near the main gate, and say you have come to register a new student. Arrive early in the day, because registration periods can be busy.

Hand in your documents

A staff member will check your documents against the registration form and keep the copies. They may ask your child's age to decide which class is right. Answer honestly, and ask if you do not understand a question.

Fill in the form and ask about costs

Complete the form with your child's details and your contact number, then sign it. Ask about any registration fees, uniforms, or books, and request a receipt for anything you pay.

After

Before you leave, ask when and how the school will confirm the registration, and keep your receipt and a copy of the form somewhere safe. Once your child is accepted, find out the start date, school hours, and uniform rules. If you hear nothing by the date you were given, call or visit the office to follow up.
