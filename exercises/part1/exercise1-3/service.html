Muhammad Jaiteh's Prophetic Treatment

Muhammad Jaiteh's Prophetic Treatment is a herbal medicine business that offers consultations, hand-prepared remedies, and wellness classes. We are located at Lamine , Kerewan, next to Mandela Junction, and we are open Saturday to Thursday.

Consultations

Every new client starts with a one-to-one consultation with one of our trained herbalists. The visit always follows the same order, so the steps are listed in sequence.

  1. Book a time by phone or at the front desk.
  2. Tell the herbalist about your health, diet, and any medicines you already take.
  3. Receive a introduction with the herbs, doses, and how long to use them.
  4. Return after two to four weeks for a follow-up and any changes to your plan.
