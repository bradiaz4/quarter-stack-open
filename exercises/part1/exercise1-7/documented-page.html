About the Location

Afra Junction is a junction situated opposite to the Mosque in Wellingara. It sits behind the Jah Oil petrolium center and is visible from the main road, which is why many visitors miss the entrance.

Standard street addresses are often unreliable here because several buildings share the same street name. Using one of the codes below, together with the directions in this page, is the most dependable way to arrive.
